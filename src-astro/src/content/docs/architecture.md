# Architecture

OrionHealth follows a local-first, privacy-centric architecture with hexagonal design, enabling complete health data ownership without compromising security or functionality.

## Overview

OrionHealth is built on three core architectural pillars, all designed to keep your data private, local, and under your control:

1.  **Local-First & Offline-First:** Data lives primarily on your device.
2.  **Hexagonal Architecture:** Clean separation of concerns.
3.  **On-Device AI:** Privacy-preserving local intelligence.
