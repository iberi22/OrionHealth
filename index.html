Core Features

MEDICAL GRADE FEATURES

🔒

Local-First Architecture

Your health data stays on your device — encrypted with AES-256 in an offline Isar database. No servers, no cloud, no third parties.

🧠

On-Device Medical AI

Chat with your health records using integrated local LLMs (ONNX Runtime). Get insights about your conditions, medications, and lab results — all processed locally.

🌍

Global Medical Standards (FHIR-ready)

ICD-10, LOINC, SNOMED CT, and RxNorm indexed on-device. Your data is structured, interoperable, and professionally certified — no lock-in, no silos.

🔍

Complete Health Records

Upload documents, lab results, prescriptions, and medical images with OCR. Structured health records with FHIR-compatible resource modeling.

🔐

P2P Health Sharing

Share your health data securely with doctors or family via BLE, NFC, or WiFi Direct. You control who sees what, revocable at any time.

🔓

Open Source

AGPL-3.0 licensed. Full transparency. Audit the code yourself. Community-driven development.

DATA AUTONOMY

Your Health, Your Data, Your Rules

OrionHealth is built on a simple principle: your medical information belongs to you — not to hospitals, insurers, or tech corporations. We give you the tools to own, control, and use your health data freely.

Free Information

Your health data is not a product to be sold. OrionHealth is 100% open source (AGPL-3.0) and free forever. No data mining, no ads, no hidden fees.

Certified Information

Every medical code in OrionHealth is sourced from official standards bodies. ICD-10 from WHO, LOINC from Regenstrief, RxNorm from NLM. Clinically accurate and auditable.

Useful Information

Your records are not just stored — they're actionable. On-device AI analyzes your health data to provide insights, trends, and personalized recommendations.

Standardized Information (FHIR)

OrionHealth uses FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) as its data model. Your health record is structured, portable, and interoperable — no vendor lock-in.

OUR MISSION

OrionHealth believes that:

✓ You own your health data — not hospitals, not insurers, not big tech

✓ Medical privacy is a fundamental human right, not a premium feature

✓ Medical AI should serve health and patients, not corporations

✓ Standardized health data empowers better decisions and better care
