# SWAL Canonical Goal — SouthWest AI Labs

## Vision & Objective
**SouthWest AI Labs (SWAL)** is dedicated to building sovereign, privacy-first AI systems, healthcare software, and decentralised developer infrastructure.

---

## Core SWAL Principles

1. **100% On-Device & Privacy-First**: Zero unwanted telemetry or unauthorized external transmission of sensitive personal health information (PHI) or personal identifiable information (PII).
2. **Open Standards & Interoperability**: Strict adherence to FHIR R4, ICD-10, LOINC, RxNorm, SNOMED CT, and Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) / Verifiable Credentials standards.
3. **Sovereign Intelligence**: Local-first AI models (Gemma/Sherpa-ONNX) and RAG architecture (`isar_agent_memory`) running directly on user hardware.
4. **Clean Architecture & SWAL Standard**: Modular, testable, and maintainable codebases adhering to the canonical SWAL repository standard and 10-item documentation checklist.
