# Software Requirements Specification (SRS) Index

This page references the canonical Software Requirements Specification for **OrionHealth v1.0**.

The full canonical SRS documentation is maintained in [`docs/SRS/REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../SRS/REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Summary of Functional Requirements (REQ-F-001 .. REQ-F-025)

1. **REQ-F-001**: Auth & Biometrics - Secure user authentication including biometric (fingerprint/face) login and PIN setup.
2. **REQ-F-002**: User Profile - Management of personal health profile, basic information, and preferences.
3. **REQ-F-003**: Dashboard - Centralized overview of health status, upcoming appointments, and daily summaries.
4. **REQ-F-004**: Health Records - Comprehensive management of medical history, clinical documents, and FHIR resources.
5. **REQ-F-005**: Vitals Monitor - Tracking and visualization of vital signs like heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen levels.
6. **REQ-F-006**: Medications - Tracking of prescribed medications, dosages, and adherence schedules.
7. **REQ-F-007**: Appointments - Scheduling and management of medical appointments and follow-ups.
8. **REQ-F-008**: Reports & Analytics - Visual representation of health trends and generated health reports.
9. **REQ-F-009**: Medical Standards - Integration and mapping of medical terminologies (ICD-10, LOINC, RxNorm, SNOMED).
10. **REQ-F-010**: Doctor Verification - System for verifying medical credentials and professional status of healthcare providers.
11. **REQ-F-011**: Voice Chat - On-device AI-powered voice assistant for hands-free interaction.
12. **REQ-F-012**: Local Agent - Personalized local AI agent that learns from user data while maintaining privacy.
13. **REQ-F-013**: Network Sync - Infrastructure for peer-to-peer data synchronization across devices.
14. **REQ-F-014**: Allergies - Management and tracking of known allergies and adverse reactions.
15. **REQ-F-015**: Calendar Import - Integration with external calendar providers (Gmail, Outlook) to import medical appointments.
16. **REQ-F-016**: Health Sharing - Secure sharing of health data with trusted individuals or providers via P2P.
17. **REQ-F-017**: Governance - Mechanisms for network participation, decision-making, and policy updates.
18. **REQ-F-018**: Incentives & Rewards - Gamification and reward system for healthy habits and network contributions.
19. **REQ-F-019**: Meditation - Guided offline meditation and breathing exercises for mental well-being.
20. **REQ-F-020**: Settings - Application configuration, privacy controls, and theme management.
21. **REQ-F-021**: Sync Service - Background service orchestrating data consistency across multiple platforms.
22. **REQ-F-022**: Emergency Data - Critical health information accessible in case of emergency (Medical ID).
23. **REQ-F-023**: Scraping Config - Configuration and adapters for retrieving medical information from research sources.
24. **REQ-F-024**: Proposals - Interface for submitting and voting on network improvement proposals.
25. **REQ-F-025**: Data Sources - Integration layer for diverse health data inputs (sensors, files, external APIs).
